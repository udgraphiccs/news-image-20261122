one 1024×1024 image summing up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026.

## The six stories

1. **COP31 close to a deal** — Antalya: a new climate text after all-night talks; approval due Sunday morning.
2. **Hong Kong votes** — 856 candidates contest 723 seats in Election Committee polls, 9am to 6pm.
3. **Gulf war widens** — Iran claims hits on US radar in Bahrain; tankers struck off Oman.
4. **Florida floods rise** — the St. Johns River tops its 1953 record; thousands told to be ready to leave.
5. **Democrats split** — Schumer challenged by Van Hollen for Senate leader; vote on Dec 1.
6. **Meta ultimatum** — India's Congress youth wing gives Meta 7 days to restore accounts after office protests.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies the pre-drawn `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
Both runs write identical bytes.
